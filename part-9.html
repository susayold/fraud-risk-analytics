02

The business tension.

Fraud control is not a single-score problem. It is a trade-off between capture, friction, capacity and cost.

01

Capture fraud

Prioritize rare but costly events without letting aggregate accuracy hide the signal.

02

Control false positives

Keep legitimate customers moving while making intervention explainable.

03

Protect experience

Separate risk ranking from the final action so policy can express operational constraints.

04

Respect capacity

Review queues need deterministic selection, overflow accounting and owner feedback.
