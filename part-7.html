02 · ACTION CONTRACT

Exactly one decision per transaction

Review is eligibility first, capacity allocation second.

ALLOW

Score below review band, or explicit review overflow fallback. No row is silently converted to BLOCK.

REVIEW

Score is review-eligible and survives the deterministic queue within — simulated capacity.

BLOCK

Score is in the frozen block band. Graph novelty does not auto-block in the base policy.

03 · GOVERNANCE GATES

Fail closed by design

  • Primary score artifact and calibration status must be resolved.
  • Policy API rejects fraud labels, targets and future outcomes.
  • Threshold search excludes final OOT; freeze precedes final replay.
  • Signed amount is not called realized loss.
  • All economic outcomes are labeled simulated scenarios.
  • Final lock requires 64/64 mandatory gates.
