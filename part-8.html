01 · WHY TWO CLOCKS

“Something changed” and “performance deteriorated” are different claims.

Operations can warn before outcomes mature. Only valid retrospective outcomes can answer whether ranking, calibration or policy performance actually deteriorated.

AOPERATIONS_NOW

Early warning without a fraud label

Data quality, feature distribution, score mix, action mix, review capacity and optional graph novelty.

Question: is the incoming system behaving differently from its frozen reference?
BOUTCOMES_MATURED

Retrospective performance evidence

PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, KS, calibration, fraud capture, exposure capture and policy outcomes.

Question: did the change translate into a validated outcome deterioration?
LABEL LATENCY BOUNDARYObserved production label-arrival timestamps are not available in the IBM synthetic source. `OUTCOMES_MATURED` is a retrospective class, not a claimed live delay.

09 · WHAT MONITORING CAN & CANNOT CONCLUDE

Drift is evidence of change — not proof of cause.

Monitoring creates an evidence trail and an owner response. It does not silently mutate the model, policy or explanation.

CAN SAY
  • distribution changed
  • score mix changed
  • policy mix changed
  • capacity pressure increased
  • matured ranking deteriorated
  • calibration or capture changed
CANNOT SAY BY ITSELF
  • why the shift happened
  • which factor caused fraud
  • model is permanently invalid
  • new model will be better
  • retraining should happen automatically
RESPONSE MATRIX
Data-quality shiftData investigation
Ranking deteriorationModel review
Capacity overflowPolicy / operations review
Low supportWait / mature evidence
NO AUTO-RETRAIN · NO AUTO-RECALIBRATION · NO AUTO-POLICY CHANGE Any change starts a new governed development → validation → freeze lifecycle.
