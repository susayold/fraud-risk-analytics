01 · EVIDENCE PHILOSOPHY

Evidence comes before presentation.

A portfolio is trustworthy when a reviewer can inspect not just the number, but the source, state, boundary and release rule behind it.

01SOURCE FIRST

No headline without a source.

Every displayed value points to a registered artifact rather than a remembered number or HTML-only copy.

02STATE FIRST

A source may exist while execution is blocked.

File availability and analytical readiness are two different status dimensions.

03BOUNDARY FIRST

Observed, derived and simulated stay separate.

Governance and post-hoc diagnostics are labeled so association is not read as causality.

04FAIL CLOSED

Missing evidence is unavailable.

Missing never becomes zero, green, locked or a fabricated production outcome.

10 · PUBLIC / PRIVATE BOUNDARY

Public evidence audits the claim — not the underlying rows.

The dataset is IBM TabFormer synthetic credit-card transaction data. The public website is aggregate-only and does not represent bank deployment.

PUBLIC · ALLOWED
  • aggregate counts and metrics
  • summary JSON and validation results
  • versions, hashes and methodology
  • claim classes and limitations
  • charts from aggregate evidence
PRIVATE · NOT PUBLISHED
  • row-level source IDs, labels or scores
  • row-level ALLOW / REVIEW / BLOCK
  • raw card identifiers or merchant relationships
  • raw graph edges and embeddings
  • model binaries and private decision marts
