The signal stack

Multiple signals are combined with support, persistence and materiality. Score drift is an early warning — it is not model-performance degradation by itself.

S1 · INPUT

Data quality

Schema, nulls, duplicates, ranges, timestamps, category novelty and score integrity.

S2 · SHIFT

Population & feature drift

Frozen bins, categorical novelty, JS, Wasserstein, PSI and KS diagnostics.

S3 · SCORE

Prediction distribution

Score quantiles, risk bands and threshold-crossing shares by window.

S4–S5 · OUTCOMES

Performance & fraud prevalence

PR-AUC remains primary; calibration and fraud/exposure metrics require matured labels.

S6 · POLICY

Decision & review health

ALLOW / REVIEW / BLOCK, queue capacity, overflow, priority and reason-code mix.

S7–S9 · GOVERN

Graph, segments & response

Cold-start context, stable segments, alert persistence, incidents and owner recommendations.

Alert logic is evidence-led

Thresholds are calibrated on pre-OOT history and frozen before final replay. Universal PSI folklore is not used as ground truth.

Governance boundary

Part 8 emits recommendations only. It never retrains, recalibrates, changes Part 7 thresholds, changes review capacity or enables graph BLOCK. Model issues return to Part 5, graph issues to Part 6 and decision-policy issues to Part 7.

Because this portfolio has no observed label-arrival timestamp and no real human review operation, the public chapter makes no production SLA, investigator productivity or real-loss claim.
