Build trust first.
Find risk second.

Validation and Out-of-Time data are protected from detailed exploratory mining and are used only for predefined stability context.

TEMPORAL VALIDATION DESIGN

Future periods are not mixed into model development.

Chronological splits are frozen from the source audit; no random split is used for fraud decisioning evaluation.

DEVELOPMENT———Portfolio discovery + feature/model development
VALIDATION———Model comparison / governed selection
OUT-OF-TIME———Final temporal assessment
DETAILED PORTFOLIO DISCOVERY USES DEVELOPMENT ONLY.Validation and OOT are not mined for top MCCs, top merchants, high-risk combinations or feature ideas; they provide predefined stability context.
