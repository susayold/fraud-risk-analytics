05 · WHERE GRAPH CONTEXT HELPS

Graph value appears at relationship boundaries — not everywhere.

Segment-specific C − A deltas are the strongest recruiter-facing insight: known pairs differ from newly forming relationships.

Graph context appears most useful where relationship history is incomplete or newly forming.Seen pair → edge context already contains strong information. New pair / new card → network position can add relational context. This is an interpretation, not a causal mechanism.

06 · COMMUNITY STRUCTURE & ENRICHMENT

Community boundaries are informative — but they are not causal labels.

Community construction uses TRAIN graph structure only. Fraud labels are used after the fact for diagnostics, not to create the communities.

08 · WHAT DRIVES PERFORMANCE

The strongest modeling contribution is not automatically the GNN.

Retained ablations show that target-encoded contextual variables explain a very large share of classification performance. Graph embeddings contribute, but they are not the only or dominant source of signal.

Ablation results · PR-AUC

TARGET ENCODING IS A HIGH-RISK LEAKAGE SURFACE

  • Temporal or OOF fitting
  • No Validation/Test target contribution
