01 · SCORE VS ACTION

The model ranks risk. The policy chooses intervention.

Model quality determines risk ordering; policy quality determines whether that ordering creates usable fraud-control decisions.

◒MODEL

Risk ordering

Transaction + behavioral features → fraud probability or risk score.

It tells us which cases look riskier.
≠
⚙POLICY

Operational action

Frozen score + capacity + costs + friction guardrails → ALLOW / REVIEW / BLOCK.

It determines what the operation actually does.
Recruiter takeawayA high score does not automatically mean BLOCK. The policy must balance fraud protection, customer experience and operational capacity.

02 · DECISION CONTRACT

Every transaction receives exactly one governed action.

ALLOW is not a guarantee of legitimacy; REVIEW is not unlimited; BLOCK is a policy action, not a model output.

01

ALLOW ACTION

Transaction proceeds without manual intervention.

below review eligibility / not selected for intervention
02

REVIEW ACTION

Transaction is routed to the finite manual-review queue.

eligibility does not guarantee selection
03

BLOCK ACTION

Highest-severity policy action under the frozen block rule.

graph evidence alone cannot auto-BLOCK
ONE ROW → ONE ACTIONALLOW + REVIEW + BLOCK = 100% of replayed transactions
