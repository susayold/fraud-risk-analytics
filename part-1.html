Governance & Claim Boundary

Separate observed evidence, analytical outputs and simulated business outcomes to keep project claims defensible.

OBSERVED

Dataset Evidence

Directly calculated from IBM synthetic transaction data.

  • Transaction volume
  • Fraud labels
  • Segment distributions
DERIVED

Analytical Output

Generated through analysis, feature engineering and modeling.

  • Behavioral features
  • Risk scores
  • Out-of-Time performance
SIMULATED

Business Scenario

Depends on explicit operational assumptions.

  • Fraud loss prevented
  • Review cost
  • Capacity scenarios

Defensible Claims

  • Observed patterns within IBM synthetic data
  • Out-of-time validation results
  • Incremental feature/model value
  • Simulated strategy outcomes

Claims We Will Not Make

  • Actual bank losses prevented
  • Real customer impact
  • Production fraud performance
  • Regulatory validation
