# Part 9 Claim Boundary

## OBSERVED

Directly measured source totals, fraud labels, chronological splits and aggregate Development portfolio distributions sourced from Parts 2–3.

## DERIVED

Point-in-time behavioral features, risk scores, graph context and model performance only when their governed execution artifact exists. A registry or architecture card is not evidence of execution.

## SIMULATED

Policy interventions, review economics and cost assumptions from the decision framework. These must always carry a `SIMULATED` badge and must never be described as actual savings.

## Not claimed

This portfolio does not claim production deployment, live monitoring, actual bank losses prevented, actual customer impact, regulatory validation or real human-review productivity. Part 7 and Part 8 remain `INPUT_BLOCKED` where the required upstream evidence is absent.
