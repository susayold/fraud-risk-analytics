01 · WHY GRAPH

A transaction sees an event. A graph sees the relationship.

Fraud patterns can repeat through a card–merchant relationship before a single row looks unusual. The graph layer adds context for ranking, investigation and routing; it is complementary to tabular risk scoring.

↔

Relationship novelty

New card–merchant pairs, cold cards and new merchants become explicit, reviewable signals.

◎

Community context

Leiden communities are constructed from the TRAIN graph. Fraud labels are used only for post-hoc diagnostics.

◌

Temporal discipline

History is restricted to information available before each evaluation cutoff; no future relationship is allowed to leak backward.

⌁

Operational boundary

Graph evidence may prioritize REVIEW or investigation. It must not independently auto-BLOCK a transaction.

04 · TEMPORAL CONSTRUCTION

Cutoffs are part of the model.

The encoder and downstream evaluations preserve the direction of time across development, validation and test.

Train on the full TRAIN graph with a 64-dimensional representation.

T0Evaluation row

Score only with context that existed before the transaction.

08 · GOVERNANCE BOUNDARY

Graph context can route attention.
It cannot decide alone.

ALLOWED REVIEW priority, investigation queues, cold-start context and analyst reason codes.

NOT ALLOWED Graph-only BLOCK, production fraud-loss claims, causal claims or raw relationship publication.
