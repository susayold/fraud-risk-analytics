05 · T0 POLICY

Leakage & Decision-Time Policy

At decision time T0, only information available at or before the transaction timestamp may enter the feature base.

Allowed at T0

  • Transaction amount, currency and channel
  • Historical aggregates with strict time joins
  • Prior approved or declined outcomes
  • Pre-existing customer and card attributes

Exclude / review

  • Fraud label and investigation outcome
  • Chargeback or post-transaction status
  • Future transactions and future aggregates
  • Identifiers without a documented use case
